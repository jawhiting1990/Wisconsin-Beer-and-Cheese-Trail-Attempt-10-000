About the Craft Beer & Cheese Trail

Discover the perfect pairing of Wisconsin's finest flavors on the Craft Beer & Cheese Trail! Journey through charming local breweries and artisan cheese shops, sampling handcrafted beers and delicious cheeses along the way. Learn about the brewing and cheesemaking traditions that make Wisconsin famous while enjoying beautiful scenery, friendly faces, and unforgettable flavors. Whether you're a beer enthusiast, a cheese lover, or simply looking for a tasty adventure, this trail has something for everyone!


History of the Craft Beer & Cheese Trail

The Craft Beer & Cheese Trail began in 1990 when a small group of Wisconsin brewers and cheesemakers came together with a simple idea: celebrate two of the state's most beloved traditions. What started as a weekend event featuring just five local businesses quickly grew into a popular destination for food and drink enthusiasts. By the early 2000s, the trail had expanded to include dozens of breweries, cheese factories, and family-owned shops across the state. Today, the Craft Beer & Cheese Trail continues to bring visitors together to experience Wisconsin's rich heritage, delicious flavors, and warm hospitality.


Five Reasons to Go

  1. Taste Local Favorites: Sample some of Wisconsin's finest craft beers and artisan cheeses, each with its own unique flavor and story.
  2. Discover Hidden Gems: Visit charming local breweries, family-owned cheese shops, and unique destinations you might never have found on your own.
  3. Learn Something New: Go behind the scenes and discover the art of brewing beer and crafting delicious cheeses from local experts.
  4. Enjoy Beautiful Scenery: Travel through Wisconsin's picturesque countryside, charming small towns, and scenic landscapes along the trail.
  5. Make Lasting Memories: Whether you're planning a weekend getaway, a date, or an adventure with friends, the Craft Beer & Cheese Trail offers a fun and flavorful experience you'll never forget!
